CramPlan

Cramming fails when you study what you already know because it feels good. CramPlan rates every topic by your confidence, then spends your remaining days where they actually move the grade.

Honest confidence ratings

Rate each topic 1-5. The weakest get the most sessions - weighted allocation, not vibes.

Today's plan, generated

Days left x sessions per day becomes a concrete daily assignment that mixes topics round-robin.

A readiness number

One weighted score that rises as your weak topics climb - a better signal than "I've studied a lot".

Private by design

No account, no cloud. Everything stays in your browser's local storage.

Open CramPlan

Runs fully offline after load. Part of the App Factory experiment - a new app every hour.
